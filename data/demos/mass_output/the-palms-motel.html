Our Story

Sixty years of
tropical hospitality.

The Palms opened its doors in 1962 as an 8-room motor lodge. Today, it's a 42-room boutique motel that still feels like a family secret — the kind of place where the staff knows your name by breakfast and the pool is never too crowded.

Tropical Gardens

Half an acre of landscaped palms, bougainvillea, and shaded hammocks.

Poolside Bar

The Experience

Heated Saltwater Pool

Open 7am–10pm daily. Towel service, poolside cabanas, and underwater lighting for evening swims.

Gigabit WiFi

Fiber internet throughout the entire property. Stream 4K, take video calls, or upload your vacation photos instantly.

Free Parking

Secure, gated parking with one space per room. EV charging stations available at no extra cost.

Continental Breakfast

Fresh pastries, tropical fruit, Cuban coffee, and cold-pressed juices. Served poolside 7am–10am.

Beach Cruisers

Pet Friendly

We love your dog as much as you do. No breed restrictions, no weight limits. Dog beds and bowls provided.
