Our Story

A motor lodge with
roots in the trees.

The Palms opened in 1958 as a 6-room roadside stop on the old Interstate Avenue. Today, it's a 34-room boutique retreat wrapped in Douglas firs, where rain on the roof is a feature and the coffee is always Stumptown. Overlook is Portland's best-kept secret — and so are we.

Old-Growth Gardens

A third of an acre of mature firs, Japanese maples, native ferns, and a koi pond.

Fire Pit Terrace

Communal fire pit with Adirondack chairs. S'mores kits and Pendleton blankets provided.

Coffee Bar

Stumptown pour-overs, Smith Teamaker teas, and pastries from Nuvrei. Open 6am–2pm.

Bike-Friendly

Covered bike parking, repair station, and route maps. Portland's bike path is one block away.

Accommodations

The Experience

Thoughtful touches
for your stay.

Fire Pit + Garden

Evenings around the fire with complimentary s'mores kits. Lush gardens with seating nooks tucked among the ferns.

Fiber WiFi

Gigabit fiber throughout the property. Fast enough for 4K streaming, video calls, and uploading your Forest Park photos.

Free Parking

Off-street parking for every room. Two EV chargers available. Covered motorcycle parking on request.

Breakfast + Coffee

Local Stumptown coffee, pastries from Nuvrei, seasonal fruit, and Oregon hazelnut granola. Served in the lodge 7am–10am.

Vinyl Library

200+ curated records in the lodge lounge. Take one to your room — every Cedar Suite has a turntable.

Dog Friendly

We love Portland pups. No breed restrictions. Dog beds, bowls, and a welcome treat from a local bakery.

Location

Overlook — Portland's
quiet side.

Perched on the bluff above the Willamette River, Overlook is a tree-lined residential neighborhood with skyline views and easy access to everything that makes Portland Portland.

Mississippi Avenue

8-minute walk. 30+ restaurants, bars, shops. Don't miss Por Qué No, Interurban, and The Meadow.

Forest Park

12-minute drive to 5,200 acres of trails. One of the largest urban forests in the United States.

PDX Airport

10 miles — about 20 minutes. MAX light rail stops three blocks away on Interstate Ave.
