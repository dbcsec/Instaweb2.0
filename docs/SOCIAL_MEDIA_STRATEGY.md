# Instaweb Agency — Social Media Strategy

## Organic (Zero-Cost) Strategy

### Niche Facebook Groups
- **30+ groups identified** across HVAC, Roofing, Plumbing, Electrical, Restaurant, and Trades General
- **4-week "no-pitch" flow**: Week 1-2: Pure value (tips, insights) → Week 3: Soft offer (free GBP audit) → Week 4: DM outreach
- **Cadence**: Monday (tips), Wednesday (case studies), Friday (industry news), Saturday (community engagement)
- **Cost**: $0 — all organic reach

### LinkedIn Partner Outreach
- Target: Agency Owners, Marketing Directors, Sales VPs
- Sequence: Connection request → Value post → Direct message with offer
- Offer: Free sample of our lead database (50 records) to prove quality

## Paid Strategy (Post-First Sale)

### Phase 1 — Test ($150/wk)
- Platform: Meta (Facebook + Instagram)
- Audience: Business owners, 25-55, targeting by industry interest
- Creative: Price shock angle — "$10k website for $399"
- Budget: $150/week for 2 weeks

### Phase 2 — Scale ($300/wk)
- Expand to lookalike audiences from demo page visitors
- Add retargeting for people who visited but didn't buy
- Test 3 creative angles

### Phase 3 — Growth ($500+/wk)
- Full funnel: awareness → consideration → conversion
- CAPI + Pixel tracking
- Weekly optimization
